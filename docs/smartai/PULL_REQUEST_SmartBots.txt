# SmartAI: a smarter brain for gamebots and hired companions

## Summary

This branch adds **SmartAI**, an opt-in decision layer that sits on top of the existing bot AI. It is for autonomous gamebots and hired companions. With SmartAI on, each bot thinks with a spec written for its class: when to heal, buff, nuke, melee, use a realm ability, peel for the healer or sit for power. Groups share what they see, choose camps they can win at, travel together and learn which places kill them. When SmartAI has nothing better to do, the old bot AI takes over, so nothing is lost.

It is switched on and off with **Use SmartAI** in the launcher. Off, the server runs exactly as before.

**Results.** Four runs of about an hour each were played back to back on the same world, alternating the AI: runs 1 and 3 used the old AI, runs 2 and 4 used SmartAI. The fight recorder logged 4,583 group fights and 88,729 solo fights. The full report is at the end.

What the four runs show:
- **SmartAI won every head-to-head.** Group deaths per fight went 0.511 (old, run 1) to 0.136 (SmartAI, run 2), then 0.316 (old, run 3) to 0.130 (SmartAI, run 4).
- **SmartAI is steady; the old AI depends on levels.** The bots keep their levels between runs, so each run starts a little stronger. The old AI improved from run 1 to run 3 (0.511 to 0.316 deaths per fight) as the bots out-levelled their camps. SmartAI's two runs were almost identical: 0.136 and 0.130 deaths per fight, 8.7 and 8.9 kills per death. The SmartAI-only run 4 still had 59% fewer group deaths per fight than the old AI's best run.
- **Solo results repeat closely.** Solo deaths per fight were 0.129 and 0.125 with the old AI and 0.089 and 0.090 with SmartAI.
- **Healers and casters live longer in groups.** Deaths per 100 group fights: Wizard 10.5 to 1.0, Seer 13.6 to 3.3, Paladin 7.0 to 1.6, Hero 12.5 to 2.4, Rogue 15.1 to 4.1, Guardian 11.5 to 3.9.
- **Solo kiting works.** Deaths per 100 solo fights: Forester 41.4 to 5.3, Acolyte 41.6 to 9.9, Wizard 14.1 to 6.8, Minstrel 29.6 to 16.3.
- **Fewer spells and heals per group fight** (7.9 to 5.8 spells, 1.9 to 0.9 heals). Fights are 22% shorter and members end them healthier, so less is needed; casters also sit between heals to save power.

Where SmartAI is not better yet:
- Unfinished casts per group fight rose 13% (0.9 to 1.0).
- Solo casters start melee more often (0.25 to 0.35 per fight), and solo swings from side or back fell (7.5% to 6.4%). Flanking is a group behaviour.
- Some classes did worse in groups: Mage (12.2 to 17.4 deaths per 100 fights) and Viking (10.0 to 11.5). Solo Heroes and Rangers were about level or slightly worse.

## What SmartAI adds

### Thinking per class and spec
- **Class files** in `runtime/server/smartai/classes/<realm>/` cover all 39 classes. Base classes (Fighter, Mage, Mystic, Viking...) have files too, and fallback specs catch unusual builds. Each spec gives:
  - the role;
  - spell priorities;
  - heal thresholds;
  - whether melee is banned;
  - when to sit between heals;
  - a secondary line.

  `docs/SMARTAI-CLASS-FILES.md` explains how to write them; `/smartai reload` loads edits without a restart.
- **A decision engine** scores four kinds of action each think (heal, buff, offensive spells, full offense). The best one runs; when none applies, the old AI acts.
- **Personalities** (aggressive, defensive, tactical, supportive...) vary how bots of the same class play. `/smartai personality` sets a companion's.

### Healing, casting and power
- Heal thresholds per spec. Healers and casters rest below 90% power and **sit between heals** when the group is healthy.
- An Enhancement Cleric is not left as the group's only healer.
- **Casters stop meleeing:**
  - they wait for a spell to recharge instead of drawing a weapon;
  - they pick the strongest ready bolt or nuke instead of a random one;
  - casts are no longer refused or toggled every think while the caster is in melee.

  Caster melee per group fight fell from 1.24 to 0.53 over all four runs.
- **QuickCast** for list casters under pressure, and for Mentalist heals.
- **Solo caster kiting:** root (or stun) the monster, back off along a planned escape path clear of other aggressive monsters, then nuke.
- **Ice Theurgists** spread their ice pets.

### Melee, styles and abilities
- **Positional styles:**
  - grouped melee bots move to the monster's side or back when they have a style for it (flanking), with fixes for rubber-banding;
  - SmartAI picks a positional style it is in place for ahead of the queued or anytime style.
- **Stealth openers:** Infiltrators, Nightshades and Shadowblades hide and walk in to open on monsters with Backstab, Perforate Artery and the other stealth openers. Before, they only hid for RvR ambushes.
- **Class abilities:** Triple Wield, Stag, Flurry, Dirty Tricks, Berserk (with a health gate), Engage against archers, Guard and Protect on the healer, and interrupts on enemy casters.
- **Realm abilities:** Purge, Ignore Pain, Second Wind, First Aid, MCL, Mastery of Concentration and Bunker of Faith.
- **Archers** keep their bow drawn instead of cancelling the draw to try a spell every think.

### Groups and the world
- **Group blackboard:** members share targets and threats; callouts; arbitration of who does what.
- **Camp choice:**
  - camps are searched by area, using generated zone and dungeon level ranges (`state-165/smartai-world.json`, editable);
  - pulls are banded by con and gear score;
  - crowded camps are avoided.
- **Learning** (`state-165/smartai-learning.json`): deaths and stalls lower a camp's or area's score, and fade over time. A party that stands at its camp with nothing pulled moves on.
- **Travel:**
  - parties meet at the formation town and bind there;
  - they travel together and regroup at a safe spot;
  - members walking alone keep clear of aggressive monsters;
  - long walks are routed around dangerous areas (A* over the zone).
- **Solo bots:**
  - rest before travelling;
  - restart from their bind after a death;
  - a death-loop breaker stops a bot dying in the same spot again and again;
  - a learned per-spec con ceiling keeps weak specs off monsters they can't kill.
- **Pet pulling** (`/petpull`) for companions with pets.

### Diagnostics
- `/smartai status | spec | group | debug`. Players can use these while `smart_ai_player_tools` is on.
- A session log of every `/smartai` command and its answer.
- **The fight recorder** (`/smartai debugfights`, or `recordFightsFromStart` in `smartai-world.json`) writes each finished group or solo fight to `runtime/logs/smart_ai_groups` and `smart_ai_solo`:
  - the group's makeup;
  - the gap before the pull;
  - a timeline;
  - a summary per member, with flags.

  It records the old AI the same way, which is what makes the comparison below possible. Writes are batched, every 30 seconds per file.
- `development-source/tools/smartai/Compare-FightLogs.ps1` turns those logs into an old-AI-against-SmartAI report (an HTML page and a text report for pull requests).
- 10-minute counter lines in the server log: `SMARTAI_FLANK`, `SMARTAI_ROUTE`, `SMARTAI_KITE`, `SMARTAI_STEALTH_OPENER`, `SMARTAI_CAMP_STALL`.

## Fixes that apply with SmartAI off too
- Monsters vanished when a hired or invited companion struck first, and healer bots vanished when their group broke up. Both are fixed.
- Bots are interrupted by their own melee the same way players are (3 seconds). This follows rule 4: bots live by the player rules.
- Stuck vendor walks are abandoned after 120 seconds. "Release" stays released, which ends a 15-minute party freeze at Lough Derg.
- Arrow restocking no longer retries every few seconds with nothing bought. It logs why (`BOT_RESTOCK_FAILED_165`: no coin, bag full) and waits 10 minutes.
- The game-loop watchdog names the step and the object a stuck worker is on, to find the cause of a server hang.

## Project rules
- No combat numbers, monster stats, XP or drops changed. SmartAI chooses between actions the game already allows, with the same cast times, ranges, endurance and power costs as a player.
- Bots follow player rules: no bot-only shortcuts. Positional and stealth styles use the game's own `StyleProcessor` checks.
- No Trials of Atlantis or later abilities. Realm abilities are the 1.65 set.
- Opt-in: with Use SmartAI off, only the fixes listed above change anything.

## Testing
- **Automated:** 3,343 NUnit tests pass (`dotnet test Tests/Tests.csproj`). They include tests for:
  - the class file parser and validator;
  - the decision engine;
  - flanking, kiting and routes;
  - the fight recorder;
  - the run 8 fixes.
- **In game:** about ten long server runs on a test copy, each reviewed through the fight logs and the server log. Each run's findings fed the next round of fixes; the run notes are kept outside the repository. Then four alternating runs (old AI, SmartAI, old AI, SmartAI) were recorded and compared with `Compare-FightLogs.ps1`; the report is in the appendix.
- **Not yet checked in game:**
  - the run 8 fixes (stealth openers, bow draws, travel rest, restock);
  - Engage against archers;
  - the Bunker of Faith range (it has none, like the player handler).

## Known open items
- Group deaths per 100 fights are still highest among Mages (17.4), Naturalists (16.1), Vikings (11.5) and Mystics (10.8).
- Unfinished casts are slightly up, and solo casters still start melee more often than with the old AI.
- Some Warden and Valewalker specs still hand about a third of their thinks to the old AI.
- Solo bots still swing mostly from the front: flanking is a group behaviour.
- Not built yet:
  - a solo and dungeon strategy;
  - the rest of the group blackboard;
  - `bot-ai-delay.json` integration.

## How to try it
1. Tick **Use SmartAI** in the launcher, then START SERVER.
2. To compare with the old AI:
   - set `"recordFightsFromStart": true` in `runtime/server/state-165/smartai-world.json`;
   - alternate runs with the box unticked and ticked;
   - run `Compare-FightLogs.ps1 -Runs <run>,<run>`.
